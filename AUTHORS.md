## Community & Educational Mission

SLT Smart PC Boost is maintained as an open-source Windows utility and hands-on educational codebase by Smart Life Teck. It is used to train junior IT technicians, students, and small-business technical teams in:

- Modular Windows PowerShell 5.1 and WPF/XAML desktop engineering
- Safety-first system administration, ACL hardening, and reversible registry rollback
- Hardware quality-control (QC) diagnostics and structured reporting
- Automated static safety testing with Pester 5 and GitHub Actions CI
